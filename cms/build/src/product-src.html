Choice was the problem, not the catalogue

People opened the app, scrolled for a minute and closed it without playing anything. Research showed they didn't want more options; they wanted to be handed something good, quickly, with a way to nudge it.

The dial became the whole idea: one gesture to move between moods, a tick you can feel on every station, and a needle that always tells you where you are.

Problem

Long sessions of browsing, few plays.

Outcome

Music in under ten seconds, every time.
