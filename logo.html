One shape that has to do everything

Nova lives in an app icon, a lock screen widget and the corner of a video. The mark had to read in a 16 pixel favicon and still feel like an object on a billboard.

Everything comes from a radio dial: the circle is the dial, the band is the signal passing through, and the notch is the moment the needle lands on a station. Three parts, nothing extra.
