Making music feel found, not searched

One gesture to move between moods. A dial you can feel, a needle that always says where you are.

Year2026
RoleLead product designer
PlatformiOS, Android, Web
Tools

Overview

People opened the app, scrolled for a minute and closed it without playing anything. Research showed they didn't want more options; they wanted to be handed something good, quickly, with a way to nudge it.

The tuning dial became the whole idea. Turn it and the needle sweeps across stations, each landing with a small tick. It replaced the search-first home screen and became the main way to listen.
